# HomeCheck

> Check South African home ownership costs, municipal audit context and police-precinct crime trends. Compare properties before buying with HomeCheck.

The current preview takes no payments. R99, R199 and R299 packs are proposed pricing, not connected checkout. Example asking prices are indicative inputs, not live listings or licensed valuations. Police-station counts describe a precinct and never establish the safety of a street or property. Municipal audit outcomes are dated context, not a prediction of service quality for a selected address. Saved checks and personal budget assumptions stay in this browser and are excluded from public discovery files. Photos and the interactive house are representative; no building survey, deeds search or title verification is provided.
