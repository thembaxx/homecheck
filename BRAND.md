# HomeCheck

**Know the cost of coming home.**

Check South African home ownership costs, municipal audit context and police-precinct crime trends. Compare properties before buying with HomeCheck.

`src/brand.json` holds the public name, positioning, verified canonical origin, capabilities and limits. `npm run discovery` regenerates public overview, robots, sitemap, `/llms.txt`, its `/llm.txt` alias and `/llms-full.txt`; production builds run it automatically. The SVG source and 1200×630 social PNG live in `public/`.

Voice: plain, buyer-oriented and evidence-led. Preserve the warm paper, sage and lemon palette, serif wordmark and house/check mark. Distinguish illustrative costs, dated evidence and planned pricing from live data.

Current verified public origin: `https://homecheck-navy.vercel.app`. Change `url` in the brand source when the production domain changes and regenerate the files. Do not use a deployment-preview or personal-report URL as the canonical origin. The sitemap contains only the homepage and public overview.

LLM files contain public product facts and source links only. They are context documents, not crawler-permission rules or a guarantee of search ranking or AI citations.
